Privacy Policy — FuelBook

Effective date: October 19, 2026

FuelBook is a fuel and vehicle-service log. You record fill-ups and service visits, and the app calculates miles and MPG on a full-tank-to-full-tank basis. Your log is stored on your device.

The short version

  • No accounts. No sign-up, no login, no email address is collected.
  • No analytics, no advertising. The app contains no analytics or ad SDKs and sends no usage statistics anywhere.
  • Local by default. If you never turn on sync, your data never leaves your phone.
  • Sync is optional and off until you turn it on — see the next section for exactly what it sends.

Optional two-phone sync — what it actually does

FuelBook can sync your log between two phones (for example, yours and a second driver of the same vehicle). Sync only runs if you generate a sync code on one phone and enter that code on the other. When sync is on, the app sends your log to a Google Firebase Realtime Database over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, under your sync code, so the other phone can read it. The data synced is:

  • Vehicle names and fuel type
  • Fuel entries: date and time, odometer, gallons, price per gallon, total cost, full/partial tank, and any note you typed
  • Service entries: date and time, odometer, service category, parts and labor costs, and any note you typed
  • An optional phone-name label you can set, used only to show which phone added an entry
  • Sync bookkeeping: entry IDs and edit timestamps, so the two phones merge changes instead of overwriting each other

Nothing else is sent. The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers, synced or otherwise.

Keep your sync code private. Anyone who has your sync code can read and change the synced copy of your log. If you think your code has been shared, turn off sync in the app and contact us about deleting the synced copy.

Firebase is operated by Google and acts as our hosting provider for this feature. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else or use it for advertising or marketing.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: delete entries in the app, or uninstall the app / clear its storage to remove the local copy.
  • Synced copy: turning off sync stops any further syncing. To have the server copy under your sync code deleted, email appsforedc@gmail.com with your sync code and we will delete it.

Device permissions — why the app asks

  • Internet / network state: used only for the optional sync described above.
  • Notifications (optional): to tell you when the other phone adds a synced entry. The app works fully with notifications off.
  • Run at startup: to resume the periodic background sync check after a reboot, only if you turned sync on.

Children

FuelBook is a tool for vehicle owners and drivers. It is not directed at children under 13.
